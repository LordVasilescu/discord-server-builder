# Grillonia Prime — Discord Design Bible

**Purpose:** Make the Discord the best friend hangout in the system.  
**Influence:** Meridian / Year 2100 — *feeling, palette, structure metaphor, voice*.  
**Not a goal:** Turn the server into a Meridian wiki, pure RP sim, or game-only hub.

---

## North star

> Grillonia Prime is where people who made it gather — and somehow it still feels like **home**.

Members should feel:

1. **Invited** — not gated behind lore homework  
2. **Warm** — soft light, soft rules, real belonging  
3. **Aspirational** — chrome and success, never cruel  
4. **Social first** — games, movies, art, AI, voice — not quest logs

---

## The dual truth (non-negotiable)

| Truth A — Success | Truth B — Home |
|-------------------|----------------|
| Clean channels, good bots, funded nights | Room to breathe, lurk, open up |
| Apex moments (movie night, raids, drops) | Standing still in Heartlight is valid |
| Ambition in the air | Belonging in the air |

**Not:** cold cyber-elite dystopia only.  
**Not:** pure slum meme dump only.  
**Is:** prosperous capital that still hugs you.

---

## Influence vs copy

| Borrow from Meridian | Do **not** ship as Discord |
|----------------------|----------------------------|
| Emotional brief (homey + success) | Full 26-zone map as categories |
| Warm gold-white + soft cyan palette | Night City magenta-only skin |
| Districts as *channel jobs* | Mandatory game lore for membership |
| Heartlight = social hearth | Combat streets as channel spam |
| Arrival → chip in → descend flow | Spoiler-heavy story dumps in general |
| Names that *feel* right socially | Pure W101 role labels or WIP zone names |
| Zarya / Iron Talon / Lily as city services | Turning every bot into a named NPC with quests |

**Rule of thumb:** if a newcomer who never heard of Meridian still “gets it,” the design is correct.

## Voice

- Warm, clear, slightly futuristic — not tryhard leetspeak  
- “Choom / choomba” sparingly (Zarya can go harder in VC)  
- Prefer: *descend, chip in, Heartlight, the Circuit, Broadcast Mile*  
- Avoid: endless `▓▓▓` walls, all-caps SYSTEM noise, edge-lord threats  
- Rules still firm; tone is *planetary law with a smile*, not a police blotter  

**Tagline (server description, ≤100 chars):**  
`warm capital of 2100 — success on the skyline, home in the square. read the code. chip in. come home.`
